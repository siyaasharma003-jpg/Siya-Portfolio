01 — A little about me

Environmental Studies first. Then everything it touches.

I studied Environmental Studies, and the questions kept going past the environment: who pays for new technology, how a waste system actually works, what makes a report believable. The management programme at Vedica is my latest attempt to understand how organisations make decisions.

So far most of my work has been research. Deep-tech ecosystems at EY, sustainability policy at GIZ, a CSR annual report at Pernod Ricard India Foundation. The part I like is the middle, where scattered sources turn into one useful page.

Away from the desk I write on Medium, swim, and do theatre and dance.
