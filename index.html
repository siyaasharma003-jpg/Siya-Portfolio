    01 · A little about me

    Environmental Studies first. Then everything it touches.

    I studied Environmental Studies because I wanted to know why things like waste and air quality are so hard to fix. The answer kept leading somewhere else: money, policy, the way companies make decisions. So I followed it.

    Now I’m in a management programme at Vedica, and most of what I’ve done so far is research. Deep tech at EY, sustainability policy at GIZ, a CSR annual report at the Pernod Ricard India Foundation. The part I like best is the middle, where a pile of scattered sources turns into one page someone can use.

    Away from the desk I write on Medium, swim, and sometimes end up on a stage.
