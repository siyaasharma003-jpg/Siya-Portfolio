ABOUT

Curious by nature. Strategic by practice.

I started with the environment. Then I became curious about everything connected to it.

My undergraduate degree taught me to think about environmental systems, sustainability, waste, policy and society. My professional experiences then pushed that curiosity into new areas: technology, business, innovation, development, strategy, impact, markets. Vedica added a management lens.

My work has taken me from circular-economy research to deep-tech ecosystems, CSR reporting, international development and venture-capital strategy. They might look like different worlds, but the question that keeps pulling me in is the same: how do complex systems actually work — and how can we make them work better?

The thread: Research → Understand → Connect → Communicate. I research complicated subjects, understand the systems and stakeholders, connect what looks fragmented, then translate it into things people can use.

Where the business bug began: TiE Young Entrepreneurs, Delhi Business Plan Competition, 2018–19.
